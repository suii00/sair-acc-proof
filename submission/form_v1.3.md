## Reference notes (optional, ready to paste)

This revises Hideto Iitsuka's ACC-P000018. The comparison with Rob Sneiderman's work uses auxiliary Miller–Schupp results in his repository at tag v0.1.0. His published ACC-P000002 concerns a square-presentation family. The path to AK(n) uses the known paths through MS(n,w₀) and MS(n,w₁) from Shehper et al. and Myasnikov, Myasnikov and Shpilrain. Fagan et al., Appendix F.1, Table 2, report the six n = 5 benchmark representatives independently certified here and two additional representatives for the classes ±4 modulo 30. The description links the papers and specifies the word for each comparison.
